Plain policies

Payments

Stripe charges the card. This store stores the plan name and the time, not the card number. Cancel in the Stripe customer portal.

Refunds

Digital goods can be refunded within 14 days if delivery failed. Larger refunds wait for the owner override.

Privacy

We keep the email Stripe gives us, the plan, and support notes. We do not sell that list.

Owner

The human named on the account is the legal owner. Agents may sell, answer, and record. They may not take on a new legal duty without the owner.
