A normal day

The store works while you do not.

1. Open

The gateway reads your variables and shows the name, prices, and products.

2. Sell

A visitor picks a plan. Stripe takes the payment. This site never stores the card.

3. Record

Checkout events are logged. High-impact stops wait for your override token.

What you are not asked to do

You do not answer tickets, set each price by hand, or watch the server. Later modules take those desks. This module is the front door and the paywall.

You are the legal owner. If money, law, or a customer harm is on the line, the store stops and asks you.
